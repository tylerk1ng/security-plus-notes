## CIA Triad
- **Confidentiality**: Encryption, access controls
- **Integrity**: Hashing, checksums
- **Availability**: Redundancy, backups

## AAA
- **Authentication**: Passwords, biometrics, MFA
- **Authorization**: RBAC, least privilege
- **Accounting**: Logging for audits

## Key Takeaways
- Zero Trust: Verify explicitly, use least privilege.
- Change Management: Baselines, approvals.
